# Boat Care Pula case study foundation

## Business challenge

Boat Care Pula offers serious operational responsibility, but the original brand materials presented it more like a small promotional service. The new site reframes the offer around continuity, transparency and local presence for owners who are away from their boat.

## Core audience

Foreign and non-local boat owners with vessels in Pula, Veruda, Bunarina and Medulin who need a trusted local contact throughout the year.

## Strategic idea

The service is not primarily selling cleaning or emergency fixes. It is selling early detection, clear reporting and peace of mind. The primary product is a monthly care relationship.

## Information architecture

1. Clear promise and direct contact
2. Problem and service value
3. Operating process
4. Concrete inspection checklist
5. Watch, Care and Guard plans
6. Additional services
7. Trust and responsibility principles
8. Service area
9. Objection handling through FAQ
10. WhatsApp conversion point

## Design rationale

The identity keeps the recognizable deep navy base and geometric boat mark. The former bright yellow is refined into a muted brass gold. Generous spacing, large editorial type and restrained motion create a more established service brand. Operational UI details, such as the completed check status card, communicate process without making unsupported claims.

## Conversion decisions

- English is the default language for the primary foreign-owner audience.
- Croatian remains available through an immediate language switch.
- WhatsApp uses the real contact number from supplied material.
- Prices remain explicit editable placeholders until the final rate card is approved.
- Testimonials and client logos are intentionally excluded because none were verified.
- Legal links are visible, while their status is clearly marked pending business details.

## Future proof points to capture

- Number and quality of leads before and after launch
- Clicks on first inspection and WhatsApp actions
- Package interest split across Watch, Care and Guard
- Conversion by language and service area
- Client feedback on report clarity
- Real marina and inspection photography
